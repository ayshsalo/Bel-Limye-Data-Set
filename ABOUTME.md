About Me
📊 Business & Financial Data Analyst — Medical Spa & Day Spa Industry

I turn spa and wellness operations into decisions — building the dashboards, KPI models, and financial reporting that owners, managers, and investors actually need to run a med spa or day spa profitably.

My background is inside this exact industry, which means I know firsthand what matters to a spa's P&L: client retention and rebook rate, service mix and package economics, esthetician productivity and upsell performance, seasonal revenue swings, and the true cost of a raise or a new hire. I build models around the metrics that actually move a spa's bottom line, grounded in how the business really operates day to day.

That combination — operator's intuition plus analytical rigor — is what I bring to every project: financial models an owner can trust, and dashboards a manager will actually use.

What I work with: Excel/Google Sheets (formula-driven models, not static reports), business KPI design, revenue and payroll forecasting, client retention and rebooking analysis, and dataset design for BI tools like Power BI and Tableau.
